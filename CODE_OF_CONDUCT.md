# Code of Conduct

sheetlint-jp follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful, practical, and careful with business data. Do not post private CSV contents, customer records, or credentials in public issues.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments that expose private data or make collaboration unsafe.
